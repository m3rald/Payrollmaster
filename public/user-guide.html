Introduction

What is PayrollMaster?

PayrollMaster is a decentralised payroll platform that replaces spreadsheets and bank wires with verifiable onchain transactions. Salary data is encrypted client-side using AES-256 — only the merkle root (a cryptographic fingerprint) is stored on the blockchain. No amounts, no names appear in public.

When a payroll run executes, USDC moves from a company vault directly to a smart contract. Each worker claims their exact allocation by submitting a cryptographic proof. The whole process is transparent, auditable, and cannot be altered after approval.

🔒

Privacy First

Salary amounts are AES-GCM encrypted. Only the merkle root goes onchain — no salary data is ever public.

⚖️

Separation of Duties

A Maker prepares the run. A Checker approves it. An Owner executes. No single person controls the full flow.

✅

Merkle Verification

Each worker's claim is verified against the approved merkle root. No overpaying, no underpaying, no fraud.

⚡

Instant Settlement

Built on Arc — USDC is the native gas token. Workers receive USDC directly in their wallet within seconds.

Roles

The Four Roles

PayrollMaster enforces separation of duties through four distinct roles. Each is a separate wallet address registered on-chain.

👑

Owner

The organisation administrator. Has full control.

  • Creates the organisation on-chain
  • Funds the vault with USDC
  • Assigns Maker and Checker roles
  • Executes approved payroll runs
  • Can withdraw from vault
📝

Maker

The payroll preparer. Handles run setup.

  • Manages the employee roster
  • Creates payroll runs with amounts
  • Publishes the roster merkle root on-chain
  • Generates claim links for workers
✅

Checker

The approver. Provides a second set of eyes.

  • Reviews the sanctions screen
  • Attests to the roster root
  • Signs and approves the run on-chain
  • Cannot execute or fund — only approve
👷

Worker / Employee

The payment recipient. No blockchain knowledge needed.

  • Receives a claim link from the Maker
  • Connects wallet to claim USDC
  • Claim is verified against merkle proof
  • USDC sent directly to their wallet
⚠️ The Maker and Checker must be different wallets. The Checker must not be the same person as the Maker. This is the core security model.

Getting Started

Before You Begin

1

Install a supported wallet

You need MetaMask or OKX Wallet browser extension. Download from the official site only — never a third-party source.

🛡️ Always verify you're on metamask.io or okx.com/web3 before downloading.
2

Add Arc Testnet to your wallet

Open payrollmaster.netlify.app and connect your wallet. A pop-up will ask you to add the Arc Testnet network — click Approve.

✓ If the popup doesn't appear, add the network manually using the details in the Wallet Setup section.
3

Get testnet USDC

For the testnet, use the "Get test USDC" button in the app sidebar. This drips free testnet USDC to your connected wallet so you can try the full flow without real money.

⚠️ Testnet USDC has no real value. It is for testing only.
4

Prepare your team's wallet addresses

You'll need the wallet address of the person who will be the Checker, and the wallet address of each employee who will receive pay. These must be actual wallet addresses — no email or username will work.

Step 1

Creating Your Organisation

The Owner wallet creates the org. This deploys a Vault contract on-chain that holds payroll funds.

1

Connect as Owner

Connect the wallet that will own the organisation. This address will have full admin rights.

2

Click "New Org" on the Home screen

Enter your organisation name. Confirm the transaction in your wallet. The vault will be deployed — this takes about 10–20 seconds.

⚠️ MetaMask may show a "burn address" warning. This is a false alarm — your vault is a newly deployed contract with no transaction history yet. It is safe to proceed.
3

Go to Settings → Assign Roles

Paste the Maker's wallet address and the Checker's wallet address. Each assignment is a separate on-chain transaction. The Owner can act as Maker if no separate Maker is assigned.

4

Fund the Vault

On the Home screen, enter the amount of USDC to deposit. Click "Approve & Fund". Two transactions fire: an ERC-20 approval, then the deposit. Both must be confirmed.

✓ Make sure the vault has enough USDC to cover the total payroll amount before creating a run.
Step 2

Building the Employee Roster

The Maker manages the roster. Each employee record links a person to their payment wallet.

1

Connect as Maker

Switch to the Maker wallet in your browser extension. The app detects your role and shows the Roster tab.

2

Go to Roster → Add Employee

For each employee, enter:

  • Full name — for display purposes
  • Wallet address — the 0x address where USDC will be sent. This is required and must be correct.
  • Email — optional, for internal reference only
🚨 Double-check every wallet address. USDC is sent to this address on-chain. There is no undo. A wrong address means the worker cannot claim their funds.
3

Share portal links

Each employee row has a link icon 🔗. Click it to copy a personalised portal link. Share this link with the employee — they'll use it to claim their USDC after the run executes.

Step 3 – 4

Creating a Payroll Run

The Maker creates a run by assigning USDC amounts to each employee. The run is stored locally until published on-chain.

1

Go to "New Run"

Select the pay period label (e.g. "September 2026") and currency (USDC). Each employee from the roster appears with an amount field.

2

Enter amounts

Enter the USDC amount for each employee. Amounts are encrypted client-side immediately — they never leave your device in plain text.

✓ The total amount must be ≤ the current vault balance. Check the vault balance on the Home screen first.
3

Publish Roster Root

Click "Publish Roster Root". This sends a transaction to the RunRegistry contract with the merkle root of all payment lines. Confirm in your wallet.

⚠️ The Maker wallet must be the same one assigned in Settings. Sending from the wrong wallet will always fail.
Step 5

Approving the Run

The Checker reviews and approves the run. This is an on-chain transaction — not just a signature.

1

Switch to the Checker wallet

Open the Approve screen. The checklist shows the current run status. Items marked ✓ green are complete.

2

Review the Sanctions screen

Confirm that no employee wallet addresses are on sanctions lists. Tick the attestation checkbox.

3

Click "Sign & Approve On-Chain"

Two things happen: a local cryptographic signature for the audit trail, then an on-chain transaction calling approveRun(). Both must succeed.

🚨 The Checker wallet must be the one assigned in Settings. Approving from the wrong wallet fails onchain.
Step 6 – 7

Executing the Run & Claiming Pay

1

Switch back to Owner wallet

On the Approve screen, once the checklist is fully green, the "Execute Run" button activates.

2

Click "Execute Run"

This pulls the full payroll amount from the vault into the RunRegistry contract and locks it for individual claims. Confirm the transaction.

✓ After execution, the Approve screen shows a Claim Links panel. Copy and send each worker their individual claim link.
3

Workers claim their USDC

Each worker:

  1. Opens their claim link
  2. Connects the wallet that matches their roster entry
  3. Sees their name, the amount, and a "Claim X USDC" button
  4. Clicks it — USDC arrives in their wallet within seconds
✓ If a worker loses their link, they can open the Employee Portal, connect their wallet, and the app auto-finds all unclaimed amounts for that address.
🚨 The worker must connect the exact wallet address that was entered in the Roster. A different wallet will not match the merkle proof and the claim will be rejected.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I see other employees' salaries?
No. Salary amounts are encrypted using AES-256 before anything is stored. Only the merkle root (a mathematical fingerprint) is published on-chain. No one — including the app developer — can read salary data without the encryption key stored in your org's local profile.
What happens if I send to the wrong wallet address?
The funds will be sent to that address and cannot be recovered. Always double-check every wallet address before creating a run. There is no admin override or undo function — this is a feature, not a bug. It guarantees no one can intercept payments.
What if a worker loses their claim link?
They can open the Employee Portal at payrollmaster.netlify.app, connect their wallet, and the app will scan all executed runs for any unclaimed payments matching their address. The claim link is a convenience — the proof is always recoverable from the stored run data.
Can the Maker approve their own run?
No. The Checker must be a different wallet address from the Maker. The smart contract enforces this — approving from the Maker's wallet will always revert with NotAuthorizedChecker.
Is this using real money?
Currently the app runs on Arc Testnet with test USDC that has no real value. Use the "Get test USDC" button in the sidebar to get free testnet tokens. Mainnet deployment with real funds is a separate step that will be available in a future release.
Why does MetaMask show a "burn address" warning?
Each org's Vault is a freshly deployed contract. MetaMask flags unfamiliar contracts as a precaution. The vault is safe — it was deployed by the audited OrgFactory contract. You can verify it on the Arc Testnet explorer at explorer.testnet.arc.io.
Can I withdraw unused funds from the vault?
Yes. The Owner can use the Withdraw button on the Home screen to pull any amount from the vault back to their wallet. Only newly created orgs (after the latest contract update) support this feature.
What if I open the app on a different device?
Your org data is stored in your browser's localStorage. On a new device, connect your wallet and the app will attempt to recover orgs from on-chain events. If recovery doesn't find your org, re-create it — the vault address is saved in the org record and linked to your wallet address on-chain.
